RoboHand · servo spools, version 2

Glue-free spool: the horn sits in a pocket, lashed in with fishing line

Same winding surface and string holes as before. Fixed with the servo's own centre screw. No glue anywhere.

Exploded view

Underneath: horn in the pocket, lashed

1 Trim the horn

Cut both ends of the arm so it's 26 mm tip to tip (as bought 31.3 mm: about 2.7 mm off each end, red). Otherwise it hits the next servo when it turns.

2 Horn into the pocket

Press the trimmed horn into the pocket under the spool, flat side into the pocket, round hub sticking out (the hub goes on the shaft).

3 Lash it in

For each half of the arm: line down the slot over the arm, through a horn hole, under the arm, up the slot beside it. Pull tight, knot on top. Two lashings.

4 Onto the servo

Power on (servo at its open position), push spool + horn onto the shaft, and screw the centre screw in through the spool's centre hole.
