RoboHand · palm, option A

Open slot + clip: no hole to thread, no knot to tie

Each elastic lies in an open slot down the back of its knuckle and clicks into a V-clip on the back of the palm.

Back of the palm, elastics clipped in

Looking straight at one clip (empty, middle finger)

1 Lay it in

Finger pinned on and straight. The elastic comes out of the finger: lay it into the open slot on the back of the knuckle.

2 Mark and stretch

Two dots 5 cm apart on the elastic below the clip. Pull until they're 7.5 cm apart, the same as before.

3 Click it in

Holding the stretch, press the elastic into the wide bottom of the V and let go. The finger's pull drags it up into the narrow end, which grips it.

4 Check, tune, trim

Leave 3 cm of tail so you can re-tune later. To release, pull the tail down and out of the V.
