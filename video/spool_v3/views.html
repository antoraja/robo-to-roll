RoboHand · spool without horn

Where the string goes: between the two rims

Same idea as the first spool: two rims with the groove between them, a slot in the rim and two small tie holes.

1 · The two rims and the groove

2 · Top: tie holes and slot

3 · String wrapped and tied

Tying: the string comes down from the palm into the groove between the two rims → wrap it once round in the turning direction → push it through the slot in the rim into the small hole → out at the top → knot (a few knots, so it can't slip back through). Trim. The centre hole is for the screwdriver (centre screw), not for the string.
