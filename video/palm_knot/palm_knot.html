Tying the elastic knot on the back of the palm

Do this after the finger is pinned to its knuckle fork. Each finger's elastic comes out of its own small hole on the back of the palm.

1 Find the exit hole

The finger is pinned to the palm and straight. Its elastic hangs out of the small hole on the back of the palm.

2 Mark 5 cm

With a pen, put two dots on the elastic 5 cm apart, the first one just below the hole.

3 Stretch to 7.5 cm

Keep the finger straight and pull the elastic until the dots are 7.5 cm apart. The rubber gets a bit thinner, which is normal.

4 Pinch at the hole, tie the knot

Holding the stretch, pinch the elastic right at the hole with your fingers or a clothes peg, so it can't slide back. Tie a fat double knot just below the pinch.

5 Let go, then check

Let go: the knot is pulled up against the palm and can't go back in. Bend the finger and let go: it should spring back straight. If not, see ! first. Happy? Trim the elastic, leaving 1 cm.

! Adjusting

The knot can be untied any time, so you can tune each finger. Elastic was cut to 25 cm, so there's plenty to re-tie.

Order for each finger: pin the finger to the palm → elastic hangs out of its hole on the back → mark 5 cm → stretch to 7.5 cm (finger straight) → pinch at the hole → fat knot → let go → check (adjust if needed) → trim.
