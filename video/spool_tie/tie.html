RoboHand · string to the spool (after the first power-on)

Tying the finger string to its spool

Servos powered on and at their OPEN position, finger straight. One finger at a time.

1 Which way does it turn?

On your phone, move this finger's slider to 30 % and back. The way the spool turns is the closing direction. Remember it.

2 Into the groove

The string comes straight down from under the palm. Lay it in the groove between the two rims, on the side it comes down.

3 Wrap once

Wrap it once round the groove, going the closing direction from step 1. One turn is enough.

4 Slot → tie hole

Lead it through the slot in the rim into the small tie hole and out on the top face.

5 Toggle, not just a knot

Finger straight, string just tight (no slack, finger doesn't curl). Tie the end around a 5 mm piece of filament lying across the hole (3 knots). Trim, melt the end.

6 Check

Then the next finger. Don't use "Close all" before every finger is calibrated (guide 04, step E).
