Status: not connected

Close Arduino's Serial Monitor first (only one program can use the USB port). Click Connect and pick the ESP32's port (the same COM port as in Arduino, e.g. COM6). The 5 V adapter must be in the wall for the servos to move.

Raw angle, for testing and calibration. Move in small steps. If a servo buzzes or strains, go back 5-10°. Keep the string slack until it is tied (with the servo at its open angle).
