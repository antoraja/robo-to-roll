ROBOHAND · WIRING STEP 5 OF 5 · TOP VIEW OF YOUR BREADBOARD

Thumb finger servo → pin D25 (column 23)

Bold wires = this step. Faded = already done. Columns as printed on your breadboard (30 on the left, USB end of the ESP32).

  1. Power off: USB out, adapter out of the wall. String slack.
  2. Push 3 male-male jumpers into the Thumb servo's plug (brown, red, orange), tape the joint, label the cable "Thumb".
  3. Brown → the blue (−) rail row next to row a (any free hole, e.g. column 6).
  4. Red → the red (+) rail row (any free hole, e.g. column 5).
  5. Orange → row g, column 23. Check: follow the ESP32 label D25 straight up its column (green): it must be the same column.
  6. Columns 30 (VIN) and 27 (D12) stay empty.
